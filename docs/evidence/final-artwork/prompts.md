Use a consistent slight top-down three-quarter view and soft upper-left light. Keep the complete subject centered at about 70% of the square canvas with at least 13% clear margin. Transparent background only, with at most one short soft contact shadow. It must remain immediately recognizable at 80×80 and 96×96. No scene, surface, border, person, hand, decorative prop, readable text, letters, numbers, logo, trademark, flag, national emblem, watermark, or brand-specific silhouette. Show only the requested single item, except when the stable ID intrinsically represents a kit or document set; then include only the minimum necessary related pieces. Preserve a distinct silhouette from the named near-synonym items.
